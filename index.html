Orange Creamsicle Cheesecake

A bright citrus meets a rich, velvety cheesecake

When I started my career in hospitality, I began in the kitchen. In doing so, I fell in love with baking and spent six of my 13 years in hospitality being a baker/ pastry chef. My specialty was cheesecakes. And this cheesecake, caused an entire small town I lived in to fall in love. The brewery I worked for gave me the creative freedom to create my own flavors of cheesecake, as long as it was not too expensive to get the ingredients. When I decided to do an orange creamsicle cheesecake, it took like a wildfire and I ended up having to make two cheesecakes if I wanted to make another flavor, the people have spoken, and they wanted my creamsicle cheesecake. I was still very new in my culinary career when I made this recipe up, and because of that, it is a beginner friendly recipe. As long as instructions are followed exactly, you will be able to bring a professional baker quality pastry into your home, party or holiday dinner. Whatever your reason for making this cheesecake, you are sure to be impressed.

Ingredients:
  • 32 oz blocks of cream cheese (softened)
  • 1 c Granulated Sugar + ¾ cup, keep separate
  • 1/2 c full fat sour cream (Room temp)
  • 2 tsp Vanilla Extract
  • 4 large eggs (Room temp)
  • ¼ tsp salt
  • 1 tsp packed orange zest, from one orange
  • 3/4 c Fresh orange juice
  • 3tbs lemon juice
  • 1/4 c cornstarch
  • 2 large egg yolks
  • 6tbs + 2tbs unsalted butter, keep separate
  • 1 ½ c Cinnamon Graham Crackers
  • 1/3 c brown sugar

Materials:

  • Springform pan
  • nonstick pan spray
  • Stand Mixer
  • Measuring cups
  • measuring spoons
  • spatula
  • cake decorating tools (optional)

Directions:

  1. Preheat your oven to 325 degrees
  2. Prep your springform pan by spraying with some pan spray making sure it is a light and even layer, I use a paper towel to make it so.
  3. Get a small pot and put your orange juice, the ¾ cup sugar, cornstarch, lemon juice and 2tbs butter into a sauce pan and whisk it until smooth, do not turn the heat on just yet.
  4. Now turn the heat on to a medium low heat, stirring constantly until the mixture begins to bubble and have a puddinglike thickness.
  5. Reduce the heat to low and stir for one more minute.
  6. Remove from heat and put it in a bowl, press plastic wrap directly on the surface of the curd to keep skin from forming and chill in the fridge until completely cool and set.
  7. Grind the Graham cracker to a sand like texture
  8. In a bowl mix together the brown sugar and graham crackers
  9. Melt the rest of the butter and pour into graham cracker bowl
  10. Press crust firmly into springform pan, making it at level as possible, if you want the crust to go all the way up repeat process 7-9 until desired crust.
  11. Place the crust in oven for 12 minutes, take out to cool
  12. In a the bowl for your stand mixer, combine cream cheese, sour cream and sugar together, mixing on a medium speed until sugar is completely dissolved and mixture is fully
  13. Lower your speed to a low stir setting and slowly add the eggs, vanilla and orange zest and salt. Once mixed enough to not make a mess, turn up the speed to a medium speed until fully combined and smooth.
  14. Take your cream cheese mixture and split it in half, leaving half in the mixing bowl.
  15. Once your orange curd is set, combine it with the half of the mixture that is in the mixing bowl. Making sure both curd and cheesecake are fully combined. If desired, you can add a few drops of orange food coloring to add flare.
  16. Pour the orange cheesecake mix into the middle of the pan
  17. Carefully spoon the white cheesecake right into the center of the orange cheesecake batter letting it expand to the edges of the pan naturally, so it doesn’t mix with the bottom layer.
  18. Make sure the top of the cheesecake is even and flat, tap the springform pan on the counter removing air bubbles.
  19. Prepare a pan on the middle rack of the oven, put the cheesecake in that pan then pour water in the prepared pan. This water bath will help bake the cheesecake more evenly
  20. Bake in the oven for about 55 minutes, the middle should still jiggle a bit but not be too watery.
  21. Turn the oven oﬀ, but leave the cheesecake in the oven, leave the door cracked slightly and let the cheesecake get to room temp while in the oven.
  22. Once room temp, put in fridge overnight to set. Then it is ready to eat.
